About Genoray

Advancing X-ray imaging
for human health and happiness.

Genoray is a South Korean medical device company specializing in the research, development, manufacturing and sale of X-ray systems — a recognized leader in the C-arm industry and the only Korean company with established product lines in both medical and dental X-ray imaging. Since entering the dental market in 2013, that same engineering foundation has powered a growing lineup of CBCT, panoramic, sensor and portable imaging systems.
